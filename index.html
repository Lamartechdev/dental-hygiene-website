Ask Jay-An a question ↗
Are the dental cleanings really free?

Yes. Jay-An is offering free dental cleanings as part of her dental hygiene training. We’ll discuss your needs and appointment details with you before your visit.

Who will provide my care?

Jay-An, a student in the Concorde Dental Hygiene Program, will provide your cleaning under the direct supervision of licensed dentists and dental hygiene instructors.

How much time should I set aside?

Teaching-clinic appointments may take longer than a typical dental visit and may require multiple visits. The time needed depends on your individual needs. We’ll go over the expected schedule with you.

Where will my appointment take place?

Your cleaning takes place at the Concorde Career College dental hygiene clinic: 12951 Euclid St #101, Garden Grove, CA 92840. Get directions ↗

What happens after I submit a request?

Jay-An will call to discuss your needs and confirm whether the appointment is suitable for your care and the teaching clinic. Selecting a time does not automatically confirm your visit. Please wait for confirmation before arriving.
