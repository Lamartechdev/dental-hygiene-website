About Jay-An

Jay-An Grant is a dedicated student in the Concorde Dental Hygiene Program. As a Registered Dental Assistant with more than four years of clinical experience, she chose to further her education out of a genuine passion for preventive dental care and helping patients achieve healthier smiles.

Known for her warm and welcoming personality, she is committed to providing gentle, thorough care and strives to help patients of all ages—especially children and older adults—feel comfortable and at ease throughout their visit.

Jay-An is currently offering free dental cleanings to members of the community, under the direct supervision of licensed dentists and dental hygiene instructors, as part of her dental hygiene training.
